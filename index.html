About

Hello there! I'm Stephen and I like building. I've always been drawn to the idea of building systems that can learn, adapt, and make sense of the world around them. Artificial intelligence and machine learning sit at the center of what excites me most about software engineering — from deploying edge inference on microcontrollers to designing data pipelines that surface real insights. Whether it's training a model to detect coughs on a device that fits in your palm or building analytics platforms that process decades of sports data, I love the challenge of turning raw information into something useful.

Right now, I'm a Degree Apprentice Software Engineer at BT Group, while studying Computing Systems at Ulster University. I've had the chance to work across different teams during my time at BT — I spent time in Site Reliability Engineering, where I got hands-on with DevOps practices, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code across a large-scale AWS estate. These days, I'm on a microservices team, where I help build and maintain Java microservices within distributed payment systems.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me watching football. I'm a lifelong Liverpool fan — YNWA. I'm also a big Star Wars fan; I grew up on the prequel trilogy and I really don't mind the sequels.
